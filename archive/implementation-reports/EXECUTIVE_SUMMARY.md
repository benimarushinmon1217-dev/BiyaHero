- 🎓 Student Friendly
- 🚌 One Ride Only
- 💰 Cheapest Option
- ⚡ Fastest Option
- ⏰ Rush Hour Prone
- ⏳ Heavy Waiting Time
- ✨ Most Comfortable
- 👋 Recommended for First-Timers
- ⭐ Locals' Favorite
- 🌄 Scenic Route

**Business Value:** Improves decision-making, reduces user confusion

### 2. Filipino Language Integration (NEW)
**Natural Filipino commuter language** throughout the platform:
- Instructions: "Sakay ka po ng jeep papuntang..."
- Transfer notes: "Baba ka sa Cathedral. May mga jeep na po doon..."
- Commuter tips: "Mabilis ang byahe. Madalas may sakay..."

**Business Value:** Cultural relevance, user trust, market differentiation

### 3. Commuter Intelligence (NEW)
**Real commuter behavior patterns** embedded in every route:
- Peak hours and crowding levels
- Driver behavior patterns
- Wait time estimates
- Transfer hub characteristics
- Local commuter tips

**Business Value:** Realistic expectations, better user experience

### 4. Enhanced UI/UX (NEW)
**Professional, startup-grade design:**
- Colorful route tag badges
- Styled commuter note boxes
- Filipino instruction sections
- Gradient cards and better typography
- Clear visual hierarchy

**Business Value:** Professional appearance, demo-ready, investor-ready
